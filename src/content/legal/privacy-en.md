## 1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

### Personal information you disclose to us

**In Short:** We collect personal information that you provide to us.

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, when you participate in activities on the Services, or otherwise when you contact us.

**Sensitive Information.** We do not process sensitive information.

**Payment Data.** Payments are processed by the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. See "Purchases and Subscriptions" below for the purchase information we process.

### Application Data

If you use our application(s), we also may collect the following information if you choose to provide us with access or permission:

*   **Push Notifications.** We may request to send you push notifications regarding your account or certain features of the application(s). If you wish to opt out from receiving these types of communications, you may turn them off in your device's settings.

This information is primarily needed to maintain the security and operation of our application(s), for troubleshooting, and for our internal analytics and reporting purposes.

### App Analytics, Diagnostics and Experiments

We use Mixpanel to understand how our application is used and to improve it. The application sends pseudonymous usage events (for example screens viewed, onboarding steps, study sessions, question identifiers, correct and incorrect answers, exam results and feature usage) together with a randomly generated identifier for the installation, the device platform, app version, language, selected federal state, subscription status and session duration. Mixpanel may derive an approximate region from network information. This data is sent to Mixpanel's European endpoint. No account is required, and we do not send your name or email address to Mixpanel. You can switch off usage analytics at any time in the application's Settings screen.

If you install the application after tapping an Apple Ads advertisement, the application receives attribution information from Apple, such as campaign and keyword identifiers, and stores it with the analytics data.

We use Sentry to receive crash reports and diagnostic data (such as device model, operating system, app version and error details) so that we can fix errors, and GrowthBook to deliver feature flags and product experiments.

### Purchases and Subscriptions

We use Adapty to manage subscription status. Adapty processes the product purchased, price, currency, purchase and renewal status, and an identifier for the installation.

### Advertising in the Application

The free version of the application displays advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may process device information, the advertising identifier and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads. Where required, you are asked for your choices in a consent dialog. Whether ads are personalized depends on your choice in the consent dialog, which you can change at any time under Settings → Privacy settings. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt.

### Advertising Measurement (Meta)

We advertise the application on Facebook and Instagram and use the Meta SDK to measure whether these ads lead to installs and to actions such as completing onboarding, starting a trial or buying a subscription. For this purpose, Meta receives an identifier generated by the SDK for the installation, device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app version, language and time zone), your IP address and the events listed above. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt. Subscription events are forwarded to Meta by Adapty. Switching off usage analytics in the application's Settings screen also stops the application from sending usage events to Meta.

### Website Analytics

We use Umami Analytics, a privacy-friendly web analytics service from Umami Software, Inc., to understand how our website is used and which content should be improved.

Umami processes aggregated usage data such as page views, referrer URLs, browsers, operating systems, device types, countries, and campaign parameters. According to Umami, the tracking code does not use cookies, does not store personal content from forms, and does not identify visitors across websites.

### Preparing Advertising on the Website

We have applied for our website to participate in Google AdSense and are preparing a consent message using Google's consent management platform (CMP). No AdSense advertising code is currently embedded on the website, and the website does not yet serve AdSense ads.

Before activating website advertising, we will update this notice to describe the actual implementation. Once activated, Google and the advertising partners listed in the consent message may process information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, approximate location, cookies or similar identifiers, and interactions with ads to deliver advertising and measure its performance. The consent message will explain the partners and purposes involved.

Where consent is required to store or access information on your device or process personal information, we will obtain it before the corresponding activities. Non-personalized advertising may also use cookies that require consent. Whether any ads can be shown after consent is declined depends on the permitted limited ad formats and the actual configuration.

When the website's consent message is active, you can reopen it to change or withdraw your choices using the **"Privacy and cookie settings"** link that Google displays at the bottom of the page. Withdrawal applies to future processing. The mobile application's privacy settings are separate and must be changed within the application.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en).

All personal information that you provide to us must be true, complete, and accurate, and you must notify us of any changes to such personal information.
