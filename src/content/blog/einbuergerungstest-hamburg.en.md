To naturalise in Hamburg you usually need a **passed citizenship test**. The test is the same nationwide, but it is organised locally by approved test centres. In Hamburg the best known of these is the **Hamburg adult education centre (vhs)**.

## Where can I take the citizenship test in Hamburg?
The Hamburg vhs offers the citizenship test through its **exam centre for German as a foreign language**, where German exams from A1 to C2 also take place. In addition, there are other BAMF-approved test centres in Hamburg, for example private language schools. As a rule you can choose the test centre freely; the three state questions depend on the federal state where you live.

## How to register
- **Contact the test centre:** check the website of the vhs Hamburg or your chosen test centre for available dates and how to register.
- **Fill in the registration form:** you receive a registration form (Teilnehmermeldebogen), which you fill in and hand in.
- **Pay the fee:** the citizenship test costs **25 euros**.
- **Keep the confirmation:** bring the registration confirmation together with your ID on exam day.

Expect waiting times: especially in large cities, dates are often booked up weeks in advance. So plan the test early – ideally before you file the application.

## How the test is structured
- **33 questions**: 30 general questions and **3 questions about Hamburg**
- **60 minutes**
- **Passed with 17 or more correct answers**
- The exam is in German; no aids are allowed

The Hamburg questions are, for example, about the coat of arms with the castle, the Bürgerschaft (state parliament), districts such as Altona and the First Mayor. You will find all ten here: [Hamburg questions for the citizenship test](/en/hamburg-fragen/).

## Who is responsible for naturalisation in Hamburg?
The citizenship application is handled by the **naturalisation department of the Office for Migration** (Authority for the Interior and Sport). On hamburg.de you will find information on the application, which can also be filed online, and a quick check of the requirements. You submit the test certificate there with the other documents – see our [checklist for the citizenship application](/en/blog/einbuergerung-antrag-checkliste-2026/).

## After the test
You will not find out the result immediately. The test is marked centrally and the certificate arrives by post after a few weeks. Details: [Citizenship test results and certificate](/en/blog/leben-in-deutschland-test-results/). If it does not work out, you can retake the test as often as you like – more in [Retaking the citizenship test](/en/blog/test-wiederholen/).
