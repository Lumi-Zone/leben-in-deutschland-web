With family applications, the demands on organisation and timing increase. If you plan in a structured way early on, you avoid stress shortly before deadlines or appointments.

## Coordinate family processes early
Clarify early which documents are needed for adults and children and which may need to be translated.

## Practical organisation
- a shared document list per person
- one central folder with versioning
- a deadline calendar for all appointments
- buffer time for documents submitted later

## Think about school and work
Plan study topics and appointments with the authorities so that they do not clash with school and work. Small, regular preparation sessions usually work best.

## Quick summary
- Family applications need clear roles and time planning.
- Separate documents per person reduce mix-ups.
- Planning appointments early makes the whole process easier.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Should all documents be in one folder?
Yes, but with clear dividers per person and document type.

### How do we deal with missing documents?
Clarify this early with the responsible office and agree on how to submit them later.

### Is a joint appointment a good idea?
It depends on the authority. In many cases, coordinated appointment planning helps.
