The "Leben in Deutschland" test consists of exactly 33 questions. But which topics are actually covered? Here is a closer look at how the exam is structured.

## How the questions are split
Your individual test paper is put together from a total pool of 310 official questions.

*   **30 questions** come from the general topics (politics, history and society in Germany).
*   **3 questions** relate specifically to the **federal state** you live in.

## The 3 main topic areas
The general questions cover three main areas:

### 1. Living in a democracy
This covers Germany's political system, fundamental rights, parties and elections.
*   *Example:* "How are members of the Bundestag elected?"
*   *Example:* "What does the abbreviation CDU stand for?"

### 2. History and responsibility
This area deals with 20th-century German history, in particular National Socialism, the Second World War, the division of Germany and reunification. Germany's role in Europe is also a topic.
*   *Example:* "When did the Second World War end in Europe?"
*   *Example:* "What was the GDR?"

### 3. People and society
Here you are asked about everyday life, social security, education and living together in Germany.
*   *Example:* "Who has to attend school in Germany?"
*   *Example:* "What is Kindergeld (child benefit)?"

## The federal state questions
Each of the 16 federal states has 10 questions of its own. You get 3 of them in the test. They often ask about the coat of arms, the state capital or political features specific to the state.
*   *Example:* "What is the capital of North Rhine-Westphalia?"

## Pass mark
*   **15 out of 33 points**: test passed (proof for the orientation course).
*   **17 out of 33 points**: usually required for **naturalisation**.

## Quick summary
- The citizenship test has 33 questions.
- 30 of them are general questions and 3 are about your federal state.
- For naturalisation you normally need at least 17 correct answers.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### How many questions are in the exam?
You get 33 questions in the exam. Each question has four answer options.

### How many questions do I have to answer correctly?
For naturalisation you normally need at least 17 correct answers.

### Do the federal state questions count?
Yes. The 3 federal state questions are part of the 33 questions and count towards your result.
