In Stuttgart most people take the **citizenship test** at the **vhs stuttgart**. The City of Stuttgart explicitly names the adult education centre as one of the providers of the test. Because processing citizenship applications in Stuttgart currently takes a very long time, you should get the test done early so that your application is complete from the start.

## Where and when does the test take place in Stuttgart?
The vhs stuttgart offers the citizenship test at the **TREFFPUNKT Rotebühlplatz**, usually on **Saturdays** with several sessions spread over the day. Current dates are in the vhs programme. Popular dates are booked up quickly; you can then put yourself on a **waiting list** or choose a later date. Besides the vhs, there are other BAMF-approved test centres in the region.

## How to register
- **In person only:** according to the vhs, you can only register in person at the **information desk at TREFFPUNKT Rotebühlplatz, Rotebühlplatz 28, 70173 Stuttgart**.
- **Bring ID:** you need your **ID card or passport** to register.
- **Fee:** the test costs **25 euros**.
- **On exam day:** bring your ID card or passport and your registration confirmation.

## How the test is structured
- **33 multiple-choice questions**: 30 general questions and **3 questions about Baden-Württemberg**
- **60 minutes**
- **Passed with 17 or more correct answers**

The Baden-Württemberg questions concern, for example, the coat of arms with the three black lions, the black and gold state flag, the state capital Stuttgart and the voting age in local elections (16 in Baden-Württemberg). All ten questions: [Baden-Württemberg questions for the citizenship test](/en/baden-wuerttemberg-fragen/).

## The citizenship application with the City of Stuttgart
The **Office for Public Order** of the City of Stuttgart, with its unit for **naturalisation, nationality and name law**, is responsible. Important to know:

- **Application by post only:** the city asks for citizenship applications to be submitted by post only – without folders or plastic sleeves.
- **Personal appointments only in exceptional cases:** if a meeting is necessary, the naturalisation authority will contact you with an appointment.
- **Long processing time:** the city currently states a processing time of **at least 21 months** because the number of applications has risen sharply since the 2024 reform of nationality law.
- **Check in advance:** with the non-binding quick check you can test beforehand whether you meet the requirements.

As proof of knowledge of the legal and social order, the city accepts the passed citizenship test – or a German school-leaving certificate (at least a Hauptschulabschluss). Which other documents you need: [checklist for the citizenship application](/en/blog/einbuergerung-antrag-checkliste-2026/).

## Our tip for Stuttgart
Take the test before you send off the application and enclose the certificate straight away – with more than 21 months of processing time, nothing should be missing. To prepare, practise the [300 general questions](/en/frage/1/) with explanations and the 10 Baden-Württemberg questions, and take a few [33-question practice tests](/en/exam/) beforehand. The result arrives by post a few weeks after the test: [Citizenship test results and waiting time](/en/blog/leben-in-deutschland-test-results/).
