Anyone searching for **Leben in Deutschland**, **naturalisation** or the **citizenship test** quickly comes across many similar terms: BAMF, 300 questions, integration course, orientation course and sometimes "politics course". Here is what they mean.

## What does BAMF mean for the citizenship test?
The **BAMF** is the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees. It matters for your preparation because the BAMF publishes the official question catalogue and sample questions.

## Why does everyone talk about 300 questions?
Many people simply say "300 questions". They usually mean the 300 general questions. On top of that come the 10 questions for your federal state.

In practice this means:
- Study the general questions on politics, history and society.
- Also study the **federal state questions** for your state.

## Integration course, orientation course, politics course: what is the difference?
- **Integration course**: a course with German lessons plus the orientation course.
- **Orientation course**: the part about Germany, politics, history, law and values.
- **Politics course**: not an official standard term. Many people use it for the political topics in the orientation course.

What counts in the end is whether you understand the key topics and can answer the questions.

## How to train efficiently for Leben in Deutschland and the citizenship test
1. Start with the general questions.
2. Then practise the questions for your federal state.
3. Finally, take full tests with 33 questions.

## Quick summary
- Leben in Deutschland, the citizenship test and the BAMF are closely linked.
- The 300 general questions are only part of the preparation.
- The 10 questions for your federal state are important too.
- The integration course and the orientation course are not the same thing, but they belong together.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Are the 300 general questions enough?
No. Also study the 10 questions for your federal state.

### Is the orientation course the same as a politics course?
Not exactly. The orientation course is the official part of the course. Some people call it "politics course" in everyday speech.

### Which search terms matter most?
The German terms are the most useful: Leben in Deutschland, Einbürgerungstest, BAMF Fragenkatalog, 300 Fragen and Bundesland-Fragen.
