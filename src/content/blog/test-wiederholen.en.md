If you do not pass the "Leben in Deutschland" test or the citizenship test, that is annoying. But you can keep planning and retake the test.

## How often can I retake the test?
The good news: you can retake the test **as often as you like**. There is no legal limit on the number of attempts.

## Do I have to wait?
There is no fixed waiting period. However, you have to register again for the next available date. Since dates at test centres (e.g. adult education centres) are often booked up weeks or months in advance, there is automatically some waiting time.

## What does a retake cost?
Each attempt costs the exam fee again, currently usually **25 euros**. You have to pay the fee again each time you register.

## Tips for your second attempt
To make it work next time:

1.  **Analyse your mistakes**: were there gaps in your knowledge of history or politics? Or did you not understand the language of the questions?
2.  **Use all 310 questions**: do not just study with a few sample questions. Our app covers the complete official question catalogue.
3.  **Learn the state questions**: do not forget the 10 specific questions for your federal state.
4.  **Simulate the test**: use the exam mode in our app to get used to the time pressure (60 minutes).

## Conclusion
Failing the test is only a small setback. With the right preparation and a little patience you will surely pass next time!

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### How often can I retake the citizenship test?
As often as you like. There is no legal limit on attempts.

### Do I have to wait after failing?
There is no fixed waiting period. You simply register for the next available date at a test centre.

### What should I do differently before my second attempt?
Analyse your mistakes, practise the questions for your federal state and take several complete 33-question exam simulations.
