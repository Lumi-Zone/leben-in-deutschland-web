Many learners do not lack motivation; they lack a clear rhythm. An 8-week plan helps you make steady progress without overloading yourself.

## The core of the plan
Split each week into four short study units: reading, listening, writing and speaking. Revising at the weekend consolidates your vocabulary.

## Focus instead of perfection
B1 requires understandable communication in everyday life. Concentrate on typical situations: public offices, work, housing, health.

## A practical routine
- 20 to 30 minutes of vocabulary per day
- 3 short listening texts per week
- 2 writing tasks per week
- 1 speaking session with a partner or an app

## Quick summary
- A fixed weekly rhythm matters more than long one-off sessions.
- Everyday communication should come before specialist topics.
- Regular revision secures lasting progress.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Are 8 weeks enough for B1?
It depends on your starting level. Starting from A2 with a consistent routine, clear progress is realistic.

### How much time do I need per week?
Many working people make good progress with 4 to 6 hours a week.

### Should I only study grammar?
No. Grammar is important, but listening and speaking strongly determine how confident you feel in the exam.
