Many people look for a quick answer: **Can I naturalise even though I receive Bürgergeld?** The simple answer: Bürgergeld (Germany's basic income support) is an important issue. But Bürgergeld does not automatically mean no in every case.

The authority mainly checks: Is your livelihood secured? Which benefits do you receive? Is there an exception? Is your financial situation stable?

## Why Bürgergeld matters so much for naturalisation
For naturalisation under Section 10 StAG, your livelihood is important. This means you should be able to support yourself and dependent family members without benefits under Book II (SGB II) or Book XII (SGB XII) of the Social Code.

In practice, the authority does not just look at one bank statement. It checks income, work, rent, family size and any benefits.

## The common misconception: "Bürgergeld once = no chance"
It is not that simple. What is difficult is mainly ongoing Bürgergeld or social assistance when no exception applies.

Not every form of state support is the same as Bürgergeld. Work, family, previous periods of employment and exceptions can play a role. That is why you should check your own case carefully.

## These exceptions are worth knowing
The law names exceptions. Particularly important are:

- former guest workers or contract workers, if the statutory requirements are met
- people who work full-time and have worked full-time for at least 20 of the last 24 months
- spouses or registered partners who live in a family unit with such a full-time employed person and a minor child

You must be able to prove these points with documents. It is not enough simply to say: "An exception applies to me."

## Which documents become especially important
If Bürgergeld, top-up benefits or low income play a role, your documents should be particularly clear.

Usually helpful are:

- current employment contracts and amendments
- payslips for the last few months
- proof of working hours, especially for full-time work
- decisions from the Jobcenter or social welfare office
- rental contract and current housing costs
- proof of child benefit, maintenance or other income
- a short explanation of changes, such as a change of job or the end of benefit receipt

The goal is not to send as many documents as possible. The goal is for the authority to understand your financial situation quickly.

## How to prepare
Before you apply, check three questions:

1. Am I currently receiving benefits under SGB II or SGB XII?
2. Can I secure my livelihood stably without these benefits over the next few months?
3. Is there a statutory exception that I can prove with documents?

If an answer is unclear, take your time. A poorly explained application often leads to follow-up questions or delays.

## What you should never do
Incomplete information is dangerous. Do not conceal any benefits and do not leave out important decisions.

Avoid in particular:

- not stating benefit receipt
- submitting only the employment contract but no payslips
- not explaining fluctuating income
- underestimating maintenance obligations or family size
- relying only on generic answers from the internet

The best strategy: clear, complete and without unnecessary extra documents.

## Quick summary
- Bürgergeld is important, but not always automatically the end of naturalisation.
- Under Section 10 StAG, your livelihood must generally be secured without SGB II or SGB XII benefits.
- There are statutory exceptions, for example for certain full-time employment.
- Clear information and good documents are very important.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Can I apply for naturalisation at all if I receive Bürgergeld?
Yes, you can submit the application. Whether it succeeds depends on your case, possible exceptions and your documents.

### Is top-up Bürgergeld always a problem despite a full-time job?
Not always. An important exception concerns people who currently work full-time and have worked full-time for at least 20 of the last 24 months.

### Does housing benefit (Wohngeld) count the same as Bürgergeld?
Wohngeld is not the same as Bürgergeld. Nevertheless, the authority may check your entire financial situation.

### Do I have to submit old Jobcenter decisions?
If they explain your current situation, they should not be missing. Incomplete information often leads to follow-up questions.

### Should I wait until I no longer receive Bürgergeld?
That can make sense, but it is not always necessary. Check beforehand whether an exception applies and whether your documents are strong enough.
