Germany is a founding member of the **European Union (EU)**. Today the EU has 27 member states.

## Why does the EU exist?
The original idea was to secure peace in Europe by linking the economy (coal and steel). Countries that cooperate economically do not go to war against each other.

## Benefits for citizens
*   **Freedom to travel**: in the Schengen area you can travel without border checks.
*   **Freedom to work**: you can work and live in any EU country.
*   **Euro**: many countries pay with the same currency (the eurozone). This makes trade and travel easier.
*   **Consumer protection**: the EU ensures high standards for food and products.

## Institutions
Important bodies are:
*   the **European Parliament** (elected by citizens)
*   the **European Commission** (the EU's "government")
*   the **Council of the European Union** (ministers of the member states)

As an exporting nation, Germany benefits greatly from the European single market.

## Quick summary
- Germany is a member of the European Union.
- The EU means cooperation between many European states.
- For the test you should know simple terms such as EU, European Parliament and euro.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Does the EU come up in the citizenship test?
Yes, questions about Europe and the European Union can come up.

### What is the EU, explained simply?
The EU is a union of European states. These states cooperate politically and economically.

### What should I learn about the EU?
Learn simple terms: member states, European Parliament, euro and free travel within many EU countries.
