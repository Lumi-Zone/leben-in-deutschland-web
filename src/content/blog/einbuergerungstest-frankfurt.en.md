In Frankfurt am Main, too, the **citizenship test** is usually one of the requirements for naturalisation. One special feature first: in Frankfurt, naturalisations are not handled by the public order office but by the city's **registry office (Standesamt)**.

## Where can I take the citizenship test in Frankfurt?
In Frankfurt the test is mainly offered by the **Frankfurt adult education centre (VHS)**; you can also take the B1 German exam there. In addition, there are other BAMF-approved test centres in Frankfurt and the Rhine-Main area. If dates in Frankfurt are scarce, it is worth looking at test centres in neighbouring cities such as Offenbach, Wiesbaden or Darmstadt.

## Registration: what you should know
- You register **in person** at the test centre; someone else usually cannot do it for you.
- You fill in a **registration form** (Teilnehmermeldebogen) and pay the fee of **25 euros**.
- You receive your exam date when you register – the test is not taken on the same day.
- On exam day you need a **valid passport or ID card**. Without an official identity document you cannot take part.

## Structure of the test
- **33 questions**, each with four answer options
- of which **3 questions are about Hesse**
- **60 minutes**
- **Passed with at least 17 correct answers**

Watch out with the Hesse questions: a popular trap is the state capital. It is **Wiesbaden**, not Frankfurt – even though Frankfurt is the largest city in Hesse. Practise all ten questions here: [Hesse questions for the citizenship test](/en/hessen-fragen/).

## Who handles naturalisation in Frankfurt?
The **Frankfurt am Main registry office** with its department for nationality matters and naturalisations is responsible. You usually send the application by post with copies of your documents; the registry office then invites you to a personal appointment to which you bring the originals. The decision on the application is made by the **Darmstadt Regional Council (Regierungspräsidium Darmstadt)**. Waiting times in Frankfurt – as in many large cities in Hesse – are long. So plan early and put your documents together completely: [checklist for the citizenship application](/en/blog/einbuergerung-antrag-checkliste-2026/).

## After the test
The result is marked centrally and arrives after a few weeks as a certificate. More: [Citizenship test results and waiting time](/en/blog/leben-in-deutschland-test-results/).

Practise the [300 general questions](/en/frage/1/) with explanations and the 10 Hesse questions, and take a few [33-question practice tests](/en/exam/) before your appointment.
