Germany is a **federal state** made up of 16 federal states (Bundesländer). This structure is called **federalism**. It means that not everything is decided in the capital, Berlin; the states have many rights of their own.

## Responsibilities of the states
The federal states are responsible, for example, for:
*   **Schools and education**: each state has slightly different school systems and curricula.
*   **Police**: the state police keep each state safe.

## The city states
Three federal states are special "city states":
1.  **Berlin** (also the capital)
2.  **Hamburg**
3.  **Bremen** (made up of the cities of Bremen and Bremerhaven)

## The territorial states
The other 13 states are called territorial states (Flächenländer). The largest state by area is **Bavaria**; the most populous is **North Rhine-Westphalia**.

## State capitals
Each territorial state has its own capital, for example Munich (Bavaria), Stuttgart (Baden-Württemberg) or Düsseldorf (North Rhine-Westphalia). In the test you are often asked about your state's capital or its coat of arms!
