For many people, the citizenship test is the last big step on the way to German citizenship. It consists of **33 questions**, of which at least **17 must be answered correctly**.

Here are 5 proven tips to pass the test at the first attempt:

## 1. Know the format
The test lasts 60 minutes. You receive a questionnaire with 33 multiple-choice questions. Each question has four answer options (A, B, C, D), only one of which is correct.

## 2. Use the official question catalogues
All questions come from an **official catalogue of 310 questions** (300 general + 10 for your federal state). Study with our app, which keeps all 310 questions up to date.

## 3. Study in small steps
Do not try to learn all 310 questions in one day. Divide up the material.
*   **Week 1:** politics in a democracy
*   **Week 2:** history and responsibility
*   **Week 3:** people and society
*   **Week 4:** revision and test simulation

## 4. Simulate the exam
Use the **exam mode** in our app. It simulates the real test situation: 33 random questions in 60 minutes. If you regularly score over 25 points in exam mode, you are ready.

## 5. Pay attention to the federal state questions
Do not forget the 10 specific questions for your federal state! If you live in Bavaria, you get different questions than in Berlin. Our app adjusts this automatically.

Good luck with your exam!

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### How many correct answers do I need?
For naturalisation you normally need at least 17 correct answers out of 33 questions.

### Do I have to learn all 310 questions?
You should know the 300 general questions and the 10 questions for your federal state. The questions you got wrong are especially important.

### When am I well prepared?
When you pass several 33-question tests clearly above the pass mark and can explain your mistakes.
