According to the BAMF, the citizenship test costs **25 euros**. This fee covers taking part in the test itself. Even so, you should plan for a little more around your appointment, because indirect costs can arise.

It is also important to note: the citizenship test is not the same as the whole naturalisation process. The costs of the citizenship application are a separate topic.

## The direct fee: 25 euros
The BAMF states a fee of **25 euros** for taking part in the citizenship test. You will find this information on the BAMF page on **[naturalisation in Germany](https://www.bamf.de/DE/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Einbuergerung/einbuergerung.html?nn=284310)**.

The test centre will normally tell you:

- when the fee has to be paid
- whether cash payment is possible
- whether a bank transfer is required
- which documents are needed for registration
- whether you need to bring proof of payment

So always check the instructions from your specific test centre.

## Which indirect costs can be added?
Even though the test fee is 25 euros, other expenses can arise around the appointment.

Possible indirect costs:

- travel costs to the test centre
- copies or printouts
- a passport photo or identity document if something needs updating
- study materials or app subscriptions
- a new fee if you retake the test
- possibly loss of earnings if you have to take time off for the appointment

Not all of these costs apply to everyone. But they explain why the test can mean more effort in everyday life than just the exam fee.

## Do I have to pay to practise online?
Not necessarily. The BAMF offers a free Online Test Centre with the question catalogue and sample papers. This lets you practise in line with the official material.

Paid apps or courses can be helpful if they give you structure. But they are not automatically better.

Before you spend money, check:

- Are the original questions used?
- Are there federal state questions?
- Is there a mistake list?
- Are there exam simulations?
- Is the content up to date?

A nice design does not replace correct content.

## What does it cost if I fail?
If you do not pass the citizenship test, you can retake it. You will then normally have to book a new date and expect costs again.

That is why good preparation also pays off financially. If you sit the exam too early, you risk not only stress but also additional fees and waiting time.

## Is the citizenship test included in the naturalisation fee?
As a rule, you should treat the citizenship test as a separate building block. The fee for the test is not automatically the same as the fee for the citizenship application.

Further costs can be relevant for the whole naturalisation process, such as:

- the citizenship application
- identity documents
- translations
- certifications
- language certificates
- a passport or ID card after naturalisation

When planning the total costs, keep the test costs and the procedure costs separate.

## How can I avoid unnecessary costs?
The best saving is not the cheapest way to study but good preparation before the first appointment.

In practice this means:

1. practise with the official questions
2. select the right federal state
3. simulate several tests
4. only register once you pass consistently
5. bring complete documents to the appointment

A missed appointment or a missing identity document can be unnecessarily expensive and annoying.

## What should I ask before registering?
Ask the test centre:

- How do I pay the 25 euros?
- By when do I have to pay?
- Which documents do I need?
- What happens if I cannot attend the appointment?
- When will I get the result?

These questions seem simple, but they prevent many misunderstandings.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Does the citizenship test really only cost 25 euros?
According to the BAMF, taking part in the test costs 25 euros. Additional indirect costs can still arise.

### Do I have to pay again for a retake?
If you retake the test, you should expect a new fee. The test centre will explain the exact procedure.

### Is online practice free?
The BAMF Online Test Centre is an official preparation service. Paid apps or courses are optional.

### Is the test fee part of the naturalisation fee?
Plan the test as a separate cost item. The citizenship application has its own fees and possible additional costs.

### Can the test centre have different payment rules?
Yes. The fee is the amount, but the payment method and deadlines can be organised differently by each test centre.
