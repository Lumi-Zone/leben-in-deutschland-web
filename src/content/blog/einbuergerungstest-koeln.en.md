In Cologne there is one clear place to go for the **citizenship test**: the **Cologne adult education centre (VHS)**. Registration there has a few special features you should know about, above all the fixed booking window. And because the City of Cologne reports long waiting times for citizenship applications, it is worth planning both steps early.

## Registering for the citizenship test at the Cologne VHS
- **In person only:** you can only register in person at the **VHS customer centre, Cäcilienstraße 35, 50667 Köln** (Studienhaus am Neumarkt).
- **Booking window:** a test date can be booked **no earlier than 12 weeks and no later than 4 weeks** before the date. Places are allocated according to availability.
- **Cost:** the test costs **25 euros**. You pay when registering by debit card, credit card or SEPA direct debit.
- **ID:** bring valid identity documents, such as your passport, to registration and to the test.

The Cologne VHS publishes the next test dates on its website. Tip: count back from your preferred date – if you miss the four-week deadline, you have to wait for the next date.

## How the test is structured
- **33 multiple-choice questions** from the official catalogue: 30 general questions and **3 questions about North Rhine-Westphalia**
- **60 minutes**
- **Passed with 17 or more correct answers**

A typical trap in the NRW questions: the state capital is **Düsseldorf**, not Cologne – even though Cologne is the state's largest city. Practise all ten questions here: [North Rhine-Westphalia questions for the citizenship test](/en/nordrhein-westfalen-fragen/).

## The citizenship application with the City of Cologne
In Cologne the **naturalisation department of the City of Cologne** is responsible. According to the city, the route to the application is as follows:

1. **Do the quick check:** first use the federal quick check to see whether you meet the requirements.
2. **Use the contact form:** in the city's online contact form, choose the request **"Terminvereinbarung" (appointment)**. The city already treats this request as the filing of the application. Multiple requests are not necessary – requests are processed in the order they are received.
3. **Plan for the wait:** the city currently states a waiting time of **around 12 months** for an appointment.
4. **Application meeting:** you are invited to a personal meeting in which your documents are checked for completeness. Personal visits are only possible with an appointment and a letter of invitation.
5. **Naturalisation:** if the application is approved, you receive the naturalisation certificate at the front office in the **Kalk district town hall** (Kalker Hauptstraße 247–273).

The fee is **255 euros** per adult and **51 euros** for minor children without income of their own. Important: your passport and residence permit must remain valid until the certificate is handed over.

You use the passed citizenship test – or the "Leben in Deutschland" test with at least 17 points – as proof of your knowledge of the legal and social order. Ideally, bring the certificate to the application meeting. Which other documents you need: [checklist for the citizenship application](/en/blog/einbuergerung-antrag-checkliste-2026/).

## Our tip for Cologne
Because the wait for the application appointment is long, you can use the time well: request the appointment with the city early and take the test in the months after that. To prepare, practise the [300 general questions](/en/frage/1/) with explanations and the 10 NRW questions, and take a few [33-question practice tests](/en/exam/). The test result arrives by post a few weeks later – more in [Citizenship test results and waiting time](/en/blog/leben-in-deutschland-test-results/).
