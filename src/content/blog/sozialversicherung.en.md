A central feature of Germany is the **welfare state** (Sozialstaat). It means that the community supports individuals in times of need. The most important instrument for this is social insurance.

## The 5 pillars of social insurance
There are five statutory compulsory insurances for employees:

1.  **Health insurance**: pays for doctor's visits, hospital stays and medicines.
2.  **Pension insurance**: pays a pension in old age or in the event of inability to work.
3.  **Unemployment insurance**: pays money if you lose your job (unemployment benefit).
4.  **Long-term care insurance**: helps financially if you need care in old age or because of illness.
5.  **Accident insurance**: pays in the event of accidents at work or on the way to work (paid by the employer alone).

## Who pays for it?
As a rule, **employees** and **employers** each pay half of the costs. The employer deducts the employee's share directly from gross pay and transfers it.

## The principle of solidarity
The system is based on solidarity: the healthy pay for the sick, the young for the old, higher earners for lower earners. Anyone who earns more pays higher contributions but receives the same medical care as someone who earns less (in health insurance).
