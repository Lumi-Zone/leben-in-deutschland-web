The question catalogue for the citizenship test contains 310 questions in total. 300 of them are the "general" questions that are the same for everyone. But what about the other 10?

## The 16 federal states
Germany is a federal state made up of 16 states (Bundesländer), for example Bavaria, Berlin and North Rhine-Westphalia. Each state has its own symbols, coat of arms and political features.

## What is asked?
Your exam (which has 33 questions) contains **30 general questions** and **3 state-specific questions**.
These 3 questions come from a pool of **10 questions** that apply only to your federal state.

Typical topics are:
*   **Coat of arms**: What does your state's coat of arms look like?
*   **State capital**: What is the capital of Hesse, Thuringia and so on?
*   **State government**: What is the head of government called? (Usually Ministerpräsident; in the city states often mayor.)
*   **Geography**: neighbouring states or important rivers.

## Which federal state do I have to study?
You only need to learn the questions for the state in which you are **currently registered** and where you take the test.

*   Example: If you live in **Munich**, you study the 10 questions for **Bavaria**.
*   If you live in **Cologne**, you study the 10 questions for **North Rhine-Westphalia**.

## Practise in the app
In our app you can simply select your federal state. Go to the home page and choose your state (for example "Baden-Württemberg questions"). We then automatically filter the right additional questions for you.

## Conclusion
The state questions are usually easy to learn. They can bring safe points if you practise the 10 questions for your state beforehand.

## Quick summary

- To prepare, study the 10 questions for your state.
- What matters is the state where your main residence is registered.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Which federal state questions do I have to study?
Study the questions for the state in which your main residence is registered.

### How many federal state questions are in the exam?
The real exam contains 3 state-related questions. They are part of the 33 exam questions.

### Do I have to study all federal states?
No. Your own state is enough for the exam. The other states are normally not needed for your test.
