"How long does naturalisation take?" is one of the most common questions. The simple answer: there is no fixed duration for the whole of Germany. Every city and every authority works a little differently.

## Why the duration varies so much
Even two similar cases can take different amounts of time. Common reasons are:

- a high number of applications at the authority
- missing or unclear documents
- follow-up questions to you
- checks with other agencies

This means not everything is in your hands. But you can prepare your documents well.

## Typical process in phases
The process often runs in four steps:

1. **The application reaches the authority**
2. **The authority checks the requirements**
3. **The authority asks for documents if something is missing**
4. **Decision and appointment**

The better the application is at the start, the fewer follow-up questions there usually are.

## What can help with processing time
- complete documents in the first submission
- consistent names and clearly sorted documents
- quick and clear answers to follow-up questions
- clear contact details and availability

A well-organised application does not prevent every wait. But it can avoid unnecessary delays.

## Realistic planning instead of a wish date
Plan with **time windows** rather than a fixed end date. That way you stay calmer if the authority asks for something else.

Recommendation:
- a personal target period
- buffer time
- a list for possible follow-up questions

This reduces stress and helps you keep deadlines in view.

## Mini checklist while your application is in progress
- save the confirmation of receipt
- note documents you submit later, with dates
- put deadlines in a calendar
- create a folder for follow-up questions
- keep emails and letters

## Quick summary
- Processing times differ by city and authority.
- Good documents and quick answers to follow-up questions help most.
- Plan with a buffer, not with a fixed wish date.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Why do similar cases take different amounts of time?
Authorities have different numbers of applications. Documents and follow-up questions can also differ.

### Can I actively shorten the processing time?
Partly, yes. Complete documents and quick answers help.

### What is important when the authority asks follow-up questions?
Answer clearly, completely and with dates. That way you avoid new follow-up questions.
