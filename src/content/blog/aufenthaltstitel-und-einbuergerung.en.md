Many applicants look at the length of their residence first. That matters, but it is not the only point. The authority also checks **which residence permit** you hold and whether your residence is easy to prove.

## Why the residence permit matters so much
The naturalisation authority checks whether your residence fits the requirements for naturalisation. The main points are:

- the type of your current permit
- residence without major gaps
- any interruptions or changes of status
- clear documents

Good documents can reduce follow-up questions.

## What the authority typically wants to see
Organise your residence history so the authority can understand it easily:

1. current residence permit (including validity)
2. previous permits and extensions
3. registration certificates and places of residence
4. further proof if the authority asks for it

Tip: Prepare a **short timeline** with dates, permit type, place of residence and authority. This is often more helpful than a pile of unsorted documents.

## Common problems
- different spellings of your name or address
- missing proof for periods between two permits
- old documents without a clear explanation
- late replies to follow-up questions

The clearer your documents, the fewer follow-up questions there usually are.

## What role do changes of status play?
A change of status is not automatically a problem. What matters is that you can explain it with documents.

If a period is unclear, write a short explanation with dates. Keep it factual and simple.

## Practical 7-point checklist before you apply
- permit and validity checked
- extensions sorted by date
- registration certificates complete
- language and test certificates up to date
- income and employment documents organised
- names and dates checked
- folder ready for follow-up questions

## Quick summary
- For naturalisation, not only the length of residence counts but also the type of permit.
- A clear order of documents often saves time.
- A short timeline with dates and documents often helps the authority a lot.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### Is only the number of years decisive?
No. The length of residence is important, but the authority also checks your current permit and the documents about your residence.

### Should I also submit old permits and extensions?
Yes, if they show your history. Sort them by date.

### What should I do about unclear periods?
Clarify such periods early. A short written explanation can help.
