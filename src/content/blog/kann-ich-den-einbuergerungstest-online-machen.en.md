**You can prepare for the German naturalization test online, but you cannot take the officially recognised exam on this website or in the app.** The real test is held at an approved test centre.

## What online preparation can do

Online practice lets you work through the official question catalogue, select your federal state, review mistakes and simulate a 33-question paper. This is useful for learning the format and checking whether you consistently score above the required threshold.

## What it cannot do

An online simulation does not create an official certificate. You still need to register with an approved centre, identify yourself and take the supervised exam there.

Be careful with websites that blur this distinction. A study tool should clearly state that it is independent and should link to official BAMF information.

## A practical sequence

1. Find and register with an approved centre.
2. Practise the 300 general questions and your state's 10 questions online.
3. Complete several 33-question simulations.
4. Attend the confirmed test appointment with the required documents.

Check the current process on the [BAMF information page](https://www.bamf.de/DE/Themen/Integration/ZugewanderteTeilnehmende/Einbuergerung/einbuergerung-node.html).
