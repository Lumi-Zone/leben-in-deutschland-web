If you want to naturalise in Munich, you usually have to prove that you know the legal and social order. This is done with the **citizenship test**. According to the City of Munich, the test is taken at the **adult education centres** – in Munich above all at the **Münchner Volkshochschule (MVHS)**.

## Where can I take the citizenship test in Munich?
The Münchner Volkshochschule offers the citizenship test regularly. In addition, there are other BAMF-approved test centres in and around Munich, for example adult education centres in the district or private language schools. Dates are often taken weeks in advance, so it is worth asking early and comparing several test centres.

## Registration and costs
- You register at the test centre, often in person. You receive a **registration form** (Teilnehmermeldebogen).
- The fee is **25 euros**.
- After payment you receive a **registration confirmation**, which you bring on exam day.
- You need a **valid passport or ID card** to register and for the test.

You will find current dates and the exact registration procedure on the MVHS website.

## What comes up in the test
The test has **33 questions**: 30 from the general catalogue and **3 questions about Bavaria**. You have **60 minutes** and pass with **at least 17 correct answers**. The Bavaria questions concern, for example, the Bavarian coat of arms with the white and blue lozenges, the state parliament (Landtag) or the voting age in local elections (18 in Bavaria). All ten questions: [Bavaria questions for the citizenship test](/en/bayern-fragen/).

## City or district office: who is responsible?
This is a common stumbling block in Munich:
- **If you live in the City of Munich** (postcodes 80000 to 81999), the **naturalisation authority of the City of Munich** is responsible.
- **If you live in the Munich district (Landkreis München)**, for example in Unterschleißheim or Haar, the **Landratsamt München** (district office) is responsible.

The City of Munich points out that processing a naturalisation can take **18 months or longer**. That makes it all the more important to get the test done early so that your application is complete. Our [checklist for the citizenship application](/en/blog/einbuergerung-antrag-checkliste-2026/) shows which documents you need.

## Exemption from the test
If you cannot take the test because of a physical, mental or psychological illness, a disability or age, you can be exempted. The City of Munich requires a **specialist medical report** for this. More: [Exemption from the citizenship test](/en/blog/einbuergerungstest-befreiung-krankheit-behinderung/).
