A useful naturalization test app should make it easy to verify what you are studying. **Look for the 300 general BAMF questions, the 10 questions for your selected federal state and a realistic 33-question mode.**

## A practical checklist

- The app identifies the BAMF catalogue as its source.
- You can choose the correct federal state.
- Exam mode uses 30 general and 3 state questions.
- Wrong answers, favourites and progress can be reviewed.
- Translations are clearly described as study aids; the German wording remains visible.
- Store name, website and privacy information identify the same publisher.

## Browser or app?

Browser practice is convenient when you do not want to install anything or create an account. A mobile app is useful for short sessions, saved favourites and progress on the same device. You can use both; the learning content should remain consistent.

## What our app provides

Leben in Deutschland 2026 LiD is available for iOS and Android. It covers the 300 general questions, lets you choose one state's 10 questions and includes exam simulations, favourites and progress tracking. The website can be used without registration.

This is an independent study product, not an official BAMF app. Compare the content with the [BAMF Online Test Centre](https://oet.bamf.de/ords/oetut/f?p=514:1:0) before relying on any app.
