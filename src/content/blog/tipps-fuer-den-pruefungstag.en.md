Exam day often comes with nerves. That is completely normal! But with the right preparation you can go into the test relaxed.

## Preparation the evening before
*   **Sleep**: go to bed in good time. 7–8 hours of sleep help your brain concentrate.
*   **Pack your bag**: have your ID (passport or ID card) and the exam invitation ready. Also take a ballpoint pen (blue or black).
*   **Plan your route**: check how long it takes to get to the exam venue and allow some buffer time.

## During the exam
1.  **Stay calm**: take a deep breath before you start.
2.  **Read the questions carefully**: many mistakes happen because the question was not read properly (e.g. "Which of these is NOT a federal state?").
3.  **Time management**: you have 60 minutes for 33 questions. That is almost 2 minutes per question. If you do not know an answer, skip the question and come back to it at the end.
4.  **Stay calm about gaps**: it is no problem if you do not know a few questions. For naturalisation you normally need 17 correct answers.

Good luck! You have prepared well and you will do it.

## Quick summary
- Bring your ID, your invitation and a suitable pen.
- Arrive at the test centre in good time.
- Read every question calmly and check your answers at the end.

## Frequently asked questions (FAQ)
### What do I need to bring on exam day?
You usually need a valid photo ID, the invitation or registration and a ballpoint pen. Check the instructions from your test centre.

### What do I do if I am nervous?
Take a short breath and start with the questions you are sure about. You can check difficult questions again later.

### May I hand in early?
That depends on the test centre. It is better to use the time for a calm final check.
